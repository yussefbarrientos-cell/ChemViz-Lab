ChemViz Lab

Interactive companion for CW 02.03 Limiting Reactant, CW 04.03 Heat Calculations and CW 06.03 Gas Laws. Drag any box to rotate it.

Drag to rotate. Blue particles are liquid, orange are gas.

Change a mass to explore a new version of the problem. Each bar shows how many “reaction units” a reactant can supply; the shortest bar limits the reaction.

Particle count follows moles, speed follows temperature, box height follows volume.
